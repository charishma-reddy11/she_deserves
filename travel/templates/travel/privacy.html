Privacy Policy

Last Updated: February 2026

1. Introduction

At She Deserves, we value your privacy above all else. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your personal information when you use our platform to book trips and join our community.

2. Information We Collect

To provide a safe environment for women, we collect:

  • Account Info: Name, email, and profile details provided during registration.
  • Verification Data: Documents or information used to verify gender for safety purposes.
  • Booking History: Details of destinations you have booked or visited.

3. Data Security

We implement industry-standard encryption to protect your data. Your payment information is processed through secure gateways and is never stored directly on our servers.
